!
KEY INGREDIENT WARNING

THIS PRODUCT LABEL LISTS TWO SARMs.

LGD-4033 (Ligandrol) and MK-2866 (Ostarine) are selective androgen receptor modulators. SARMs are not approved by the U.S. FDA for bodybuilding or dietary-supplement use. They have been associated with serious potential risks, including liver injury and cardiovascular or hormonal effects. Long-term safety is not established.

01

PACKAGE CAUTION

The package states: not for use by persons under 18, pregnant or nursing women. Individuals with medical conditions should consult a healthcare provider before use. Keep out of reach of children.

02

SPORT & ANTI-DOPING

LGD-4033 and Ostarine are prohibited at all times under the World Anti-Doping Agency (WADA) Prohibited List. Athletes subject to testing should take this into account.

03

APPROVAL & CERTIFICATION

The supplied images do not verify regulator approval, GMP certification, independent laboratory testing or product-specific authorization. Do not publish these claims without verifiable documentation.

PLEASE TAKE CARE

Speak with a qualified healthcare professional before considering a product containing SARMs. If you have already taken it and feel unwell, seek medical advice promptly.

FREQUENTLY ASKED

GOOD TO
KNOW.

What is the serving size shown on the pack?＋

The Supplement Facts panel states that the amounts are per serving of two tablets. The front of the package indicates 60 tablets.

Which ingredients are listed first?＋

LGD-4033 (Ligandrol) at 10 mg and MK-2866 (Ostarine) at 15 mg per two-tablet serving.

Does this website verify approval or certification?＋

No. The supplied images do not establish regulatory approval, GMP certification or independent laboratory testing. Request current, product-specific documentation and verify it with the issuing organization.

Is this product suitable for tested athletes?＋

The label lists LGD-4033 and MK-2866, which are prohibited under WADA rules. Athletes subject to anti-doping regulations should not assume this product is permitted.

Are the product's advertised results guaranteed?＋

No. The product goals shown on the packaging are marketing statements. This page does not claim that the product will increase appetite, energy, muscle mass or body weight.
